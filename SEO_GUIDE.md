## 🚀 Post-Deployment SEO Tasks

### Immediate Actions (Week 1)
1. **Google Search Console**
   - Submit sitemap: `https://leecountyhub.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Verify ownership via HTML tag or DNS
   - Request indexing for main pages

2. **Google Business Profile**
   - Create/claim business listing
   - Add website URL
   - Use consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)

3. **Bing Webmaster Tools**
   - Submit sitemap
   - Verify ownership

### Short-term (Month 1)
4. **Create Social Media Presence**
   - Facebook business page
   - Instagram profile  
   - Link back to directory

5. **Get Initial Backlinks**
   - Lee County Chamber of Commerce
   - Local Virginia business directories
   - Submit to Google My Business

6. **Content Creation**
   - Blog posts about Lee County businesses
   - Business spotlights
   - Local events coverage

### Ongoing Optimization
7. **Performance Monitoring**
   - Track rankings for "Lee County businesses"
   - Monitor traffic in Google Analytics
   - Check Core Web Vitals

8. **Local Citations**
   - Yelp

9. **Content Updates**
